# Mutual funds on a platform like Groww (FAQ)

Educational answers only. Not a scheme recommendation.

**What is a SIP?**  
A systematic investment plan invests a fixed amount on a schedule (for example monthly) into a mutual fund. It is a way to buy units over time, not a guarantee of profit.

**What is NAV?**  
Net asset value is the per-unit value of the fund’s assets minus liabilities, published on a schedule. Your buy and sell prices for units are tied to NAV and cut-off times, not to an intraday stock ticker.

**Lump sum vs SIP?**  
Lump sum is one investment. SIP is repeated investments. Which is “better” depends on cash flow and risk — this bot will not choose for you.

**Can I lose money?**  
Yes. Equity and many hybrid funds can fall. Even debt funds can fall if rates move or a credit event happens.

**What is an expense ratio?**  
An annual cost charged inside the fund. Higher costs reduce what you keep, all else equal. This corpus does not list live expense ratios for named schemes.

**Direct vs regular plans?**  
Direct plans typically have no distributor commission in the expense ratio. Regular plans may. A platform like Groww often highlights direct plans. Check the official app for what you are actually buying.

**Exit load?**  
Some schemes charge a fee if you redeem too soon. Read the scheme documents in the official app; this demo does not store every scheme’s load table.
