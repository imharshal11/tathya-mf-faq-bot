# Stocks on a platform like Groww (FAQ)

Educational. Not stock tips.

**What do I need to trade stocks?**  
Typically a trading account, a demat account to hold shares, and a linked bank account. KYC must be complete in the official app.

**When can I buy or sell?**  
During exchange market hours, except special sessions the exchange announces. This bot does not know today’s holiday calendar.

**What is a quote or LTP?**  
Last traded price and related quote fields live in market data. This knowledge base does not include live or delayed prices. If you ask for “price of X today,” the demo should refuse.

**Intraday vs delivery?**  
Delivery means you intend to take shares into demat. Intraday means you square off the same day (product rules and margins are set by the broker and exchange). This corpus does not explain every margin percentage.

**Can I lose more than I invest?**  
For a simple cash delivery buy, your shares can fall toward zero, so you can lose most of what you paid. Derivatives and leveraged products (not covered here) can have different risk. Do not treat this FAQ as complete risk disclosure.

**Dividends?**  
If a company declares a dividend and you hold the stock on the relevant date, you may receive it in the bank or as specified. This bot cannot forecast dividends.
