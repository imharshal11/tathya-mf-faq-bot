# Product categories on a platform like Groww

High-level FAQ for the class demo. Product lists change in real life; this file is a snapshot of categories we teach, not a live catalog.

## Stocks

You can search listed companies, see a quote (in the real app — not in this bot), and place buy or sell orders during market hours, subject to exchange and broker rules. You need a trading account and demat account. Settlement follows exchange cycles. This knowledge base does not include live prices.

## Mutual funds

You can browse funds by category, see factsheet-style fields (in the real app), and invest a lump sum or start a SIP. Redemption means selling units back to the fund at the applicable NAV after cut-off rules. Exit loads and tax treatment depend on the scheme; this corpus does not compute tax.

## Fixed deposits (FDs)

Some investment apps let you book FDs with partner banks or issuers. You choose amount and tenure; interest and premature-withdrawal rules belong to the issuer. FDs are not the same as equity mutual funds: the return is typically stated in advance, but issuer risk still exists. This bot cannot show today’s FD rates.

## Other products you might hear about

Platforms may also mention IPOs, ETFs, or US stocks. If a product is not described in these markdown files, the demo bot should say it is not in the knowledge base rather than inventing eligibility or fees.
