# Mutual Fund Basics

## Expense Ratio
Expense Ratio is the annual fee charged by a mutual fund scheme to manage your investment. It is expressed as a percentage of the fund's average net assets and is deducted daily from the NAV.

## Exit Load
Exit Load is a fee charged when you redeem (sell) your mutual fund units before a specified period. It is designed to discourage short-term trading and is usually a percentage of the redemption value.

## SIP
SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (e.g., monthly) in a mutual fund scheme. It helps in rupee cost averaging and disciplined investing.

## Lump Sum
Lump Sum is a one-time investment of a large amount in a mutual fund scheme, as opposed to regular investments through SIP.

## ELSS
ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) is a category of equity mutual funds that offers tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, with a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years.

## Lock-in Period
Lock-in Period is the minimum duration for which you must stay invested in a mutual fund scheme before you can redeem your units without restrictions. ELSS funds have a 3-year lock-in.

## Riskometer
Riskometer is a standardized risk indicator that classifies mutual fund schemes into risk levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and Very High, based on the scheme's portfolio.

## Benchmark
Benchmark is a market index (e.g., NIFTY 50, BSE 250 SmallCap) against which a mutual fund scheme's performance is measured. It helps investors compare the fund's returns to the broader market.

## AUM
AUM (Assets Under Management) is the total market value of all investments managed by a mutual fund scheme on behalf of its investors. It indicates the fund's size and popularity.

## NAV
NAV (Net Asset Value) is the per-unit price of a mutual fund scheme, calculated daily as (Total Assets - Total Liabilities) / Number of Outstanding Units. It is the price at which you buy or sell units.

## Direct Plan vs Regular Plan
Direct Plan is bought directly from the fund house (AMC) with no distributor commission, resulting in a lower expense ratio. Regular Plan is bought through a distributor/broker who earns a commission, leading to a higher expense ratio.

## Stamp Duty
Stamp Duty is a government tax levied on the issuance of mutual fund units at the time of purchase. As per current rules, it is 0.005% of the investment amount for all mutual fund purchases.
