# How investing on an app like Groww usually works

Generic step-by-step for education. Exact screens and legal agreements are only in the official app.

1. **Download / sign up.** You create a login with a phone number or email. This bot must never ask for your password, OTP, or PIN.
2. **KYC.** Indian brokers collect identity and address details as required by regulation. This demo does not perform KYC and does not store personal documents.
3. **Link a bank account.** Money moves in and out through a bank account you control. The chatbot cannot see balances.
4. **Add money.** You transfer funds from the bank to the platform’s account or payment flow. Timings depend on payment rails (UPI, net banking, and so on).
5. **Choose a product.** Stock, mutual fund, or FD as described in `products.md`.
6. **Place an investment.** You confirm quantity or amount. The real app sends the order to the exchange or the registrar / AMC / FD issuer.
7. **Track holdings.** The official app shows positions, average price, and P&amp;L. This RAG bot has no account API, so it cannot answer “what is my portfolio worth?”
8. **Withdraw.** You redeem funds or sell stocks and withdraw to the linked bank account after settlement.

If something fails (payment pending, KYC rejected, order rejected), only the official support channels and in-app tickets can see your case. This classroom bot cannot reset passwords or file complaints.
