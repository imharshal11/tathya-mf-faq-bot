# What Groww is (class notes)

These notes are original educational material for a RAG demo. They are not official Groww documentation and are not affiliated with Groww.

Groww is an Indian investment platform that people use to start and manage investments from a phone or computer. In this knowledge base, “Groww” means that kind of brokerage / investment app: a place to open an account, complete KYC, and then buy and hold products such as stocks, mutual funds, and fixed deposits (FDs).

Typical reasons someone opens an account on a platform like Groww:

- Put surplus savings into market-linked products instead of only a savings account.
- Buy units of mutual funds with a lump sum or a monthly SIP (systematic investment plan).
- Buy shares of listed companies on the stock exchanges.
- Park money in FDs offered through the app for a stated tenure and interest rate.

Groww is an intermediary. It does not print currency, set RBI policy, or guarantee that any stock or fund will go up. Market products can fall in value. FDs from a bank or NBFC follow that issuer’s terms, not a promise from this chatbot.

This demo bot can only discuss ideas that appear in the `corpus/` files. It cannot open an account, see your holdings, or place an order.
