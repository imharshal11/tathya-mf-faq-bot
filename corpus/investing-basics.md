# Investing basics used in this demo

Short literacy notes so retrieval has overlapping language with beginner questions.

**Risk and return.** Higher expected return usually comes with higher chance of losing money in the short run. Savings accounts are relatively stable but may not beat inflation after tax. Equity (stocks and equity funds) can swing.

**Diversification.** Spreading money across uncorrelated assets can reduce the damage from one bad stock. Mutual funds are one way to diversify; they are not magic.

**Time horizon.** Money needed in a few months is a poor fit for volatile equity. Long horizons still do not guarantee a profit.

**Costs.** Brokerage, expense ratios, exit loads, and spread all matter. This corpus does not quote Groww’s live fee schedule; fee tables belong in the official app.

**Inflation.** If prices of goods rise faster than your return, purchasing power falls even if the account number goes up.

**Emergency fund.** Many educators suggest keeping short-term cash for emergencies before taking market risk. That is a general idea, not a prescription for your household.

These basics are for explaining RAG answers in class. They are not a financial plan.
